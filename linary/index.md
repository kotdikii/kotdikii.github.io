# Linary: One Line Puzzle

Linary is a "one line" logic puzzle game for Android. Draw a line from 1 through all the numbers in order, visiting every cell of the grid exactly once. The rule fits in one sentence, and the 8×8 puzzles will make you really think.

**Get it on:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kotdikii.linary) · [RuStore](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.kotdikii.linary)

## What's inside

- **Daily puzzle.** A new puzzle every day, the same for every player. Keep your streak alive, and find missed days in the calendar archive.
- **Path.** 10 chapters and 240 levels. Every chapter adds a new rule: walls, ice, spinners, keys and locks, bridges, one-way arrows, and Hardcore with everything at once.
- **Rush.** Three minutes: how many puzzles can you solve?
- **Endless mode.** Six levels from Warm-up to Master, with a best time on each.
- **Hints** that continue your own line, a line that plays notes as you draw it, line styles you unlock by playing, dark and light themes, ten languages.

## Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Linary unlocks **achievements** there (for example, a 7-day streak or a completed chapter) and submits your **Rush record** to a leaderboard. Sign-in is handled by Google Play Games itself; Linary never sees your password.

Linary uses Google Play Games only for achievements and the Rush leaderboard. Only achievement unlocks and your best Rush score are sent. Your puzzles, solutions, times and settings stay on your device. Play Games is optional: without it, the game works the same.

## Your data

Linary has no accounts of its own, no server of its own and no analytics. Your progress is stored on your device. The game is free and shows ads from the Yandex Advertising Network. Full details are in the privacy policy.
