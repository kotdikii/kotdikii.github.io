## 4. The daily puzzle and the streak

- **The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and changes at midnight**, local time. The App checks the date online (see the Privacy Policy). Until the date has been checked, daily puzzles do not open, and the rest of the game is available.
- **The streak is kept by puzzles solved on their own day.** A past day's puzzle can be solved from the calendar, but that does not extend the streak.
- **A streak freeze** is the right to save your streak for yesterday's missed day. You can get one for watching a rewarded video. It is available only for streaks of 3 days or more, and no more than once every 7 days. A freeze keeps the streak only if yesterday's puzzle is solved before today ends. If you do not solve it in time, the right expires.
- Daily puzzles ship with the App. If the installed version has no puzzle for today, the App will ask you to update.
