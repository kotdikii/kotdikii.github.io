## 5. Shared boards

A board can be opened up to other people. Sharing rests entirely on Google Drive: the board lives as **a single file on the Drive of the person who shared it** (the "board owner"), Google Drive grants and revokes the permissions, and the App only reads and writes that file. The App has no server, no accounts and no mailing of its own; invitation emails are sent by Google.

- **The board owner is whoever holds the file.** They invite members, revoke access and may delete the file. If the file is deleted or access is revoked, the board stops updating for everyone else; whatever already reached their devices stays there, and the App cannot erase it.
- **Every member sees the whole board.** Inviting someone opens the board to them entirely — tasks, notes, due dates and hours. There are no permissions for an individual task or column.
- **Whoever invites is responsible for the invitation.** You decide who gets the board and you answer for what you put in it, including information about third parties. Do not place anything in a shared board that you are not entitled to show its members.
- **The Developer is not a party to your relationship with the members** and does not settle disputes about access: both the file and the permissions live on Google Drive's side, not with the Developer.
- **Exchange is not instant.** It happens when you open the App or the board, or pull the board down; the App does not run in the background. Two people may edit the same task at once — in that case the later edit wins, field by changed field. The App gives no guarantee that every edit survives simultaneous work.
- **Shared boards require a Google account** — yours and each member's. Your relationship with Google over that account is between you and Google.

## 6. Reminders and the timer

**A reminder may arrive later than the time you set.** The App asks for the exact alarm permission, but it is optional: without it a reminder is scheduled the ordinary way and the system may shift it to save battery. Your device's power-saving mode may delay it further. Reminders also will not arrive if you did not grant the notification permission.

**Time tracking does not run in the background.** The timer is two timestamps in a database, not a background counter: it survives closing the App and restarting the device, but the App does not watch you and counts nothing while you are not using it.

**The App is not a legal time-tracking instrument.** Reports and exports are informational; whether they are suitable for settlements with a client or an employer is for you to decide. The same applies to hours recorded against other members on a shared board.
